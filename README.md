## The entry (126 characters, one line)

> IT 'fixed' my laptop by rebooting it — closing my 47 tabs, my unsaved draft, and apparently my will to meet Friday's deadline.

## Why it works

Everyone has lived the "IT fixed it" reboot: 47 tabs and an unsaved draft vanish in one click, and the punchline escalates from lost work to lost willpower — the deadline is still Friday, but the motivation is gone. Concrete numbers, no office cliché, lands on one read.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network, no clock) gate-checks the line (1–200 chars, single line, no leading/trailing whitespace) and writes it — with no trailing newline — to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`).

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```
